How inkJam met you

A rom-com in seven scenes, with a little making-of on the side.

Start the story

Dear Reader,

Everything below is true, apart from the parts that are made up. inkJam is a real little blogging site that was built one line at a time. This is how it was made, and how it ended up meeting you. Grab a chai.

Scene 1 · Cold open

A blank page, 2 a.m.

In the beginning there was an empty file called index.html and a very quiet inkwell. It had a quill, plenty of ink and absolutely nobody to write to.

“Someday,” said the inkwell, “someone will turn up with a story.” It named itself inkJam: ink for the plan, jam for the mood. A little sweet, a little sticky, best shared.

Behind the scenesinkJam started as an empty folder with an index.html, a css folder, a js folder and an img folder. The inkwell-and-quill drawing became its logo.
Scene 2 · The meet-cute

Oops, you walked in on the headline

You arrived the way everyone does: by accident, a little curious, half-expecting a pop-up. inkJam was mid-rehearsal, rearranging its own buttons, when it spotted you in the doorway and knocked over the “Get started” button. Ink everywhere.

You laughed. inkJam blushed in a very specific shade of blue (#3858e9) and said the first thing that came to mind: “Blogging, made yours.” It had practised something smoother.

Behind the scenesThe sign-up page is the blue landing page you saw first: a big serif headline, a short promise and a form card. Bootstrap handles the layout, and JavaScript checks every field before you can sign up.
Scene 3 · The no-pressure first date

Guest mode

You weren’t ready to give your name, and inkJam understood. “Guest mode,” it said, sliding a button into the top-left corner where you couldn’t miss it. “Look around. Read anything. No passwords, no strings.”

So you wandered. You read about chai, SQL joins and a very ambitious weekend in Kasol. Whenever you reached for the pen, a polite sign-up page tapped your shoulder. “Not yet,” it said gently. “Come back when you’re ready.”

Behind the scenesGuest mode opens home.html in read-only mode. The +, the hearts, Create post and the forms all send guests to the sign-up page, so nobody writes anonymously.
Scene 4 · The montage

Categories, chai and a search bar

Cue the music. You and inkJam spent a rainy afternoon in Food, lost an hour in Books, argued about playlists in Music and packed a bag in Travel. Default turned out to be the friend who shows up to everything.

Bootstrap, inkJam’s best friend, kept every column in line. Font Awesome handed out tiny icons like jewellery. JavaScript brought the spark: filters that answered instantly and a search bar you fell for a little.

Behind the scenesThe header and footer are written once, in header.js and footer.js, and appear on every page. The category pane and search live in home.js, and your hearts are remembered in your browser.
Scene 5 · The misunderstanding

You clicked the plus sign

Every rom-com has the part where it all goes sideways. You tapped the + to write your first post and landed straight back on the sign-up page. “Are you ghosting me?” you asked. “I’m protecting your draft!” said inkJam, and then, much more quietly: “I just want to know who’s writing.”

That night inkJam typed eleven apologies into the contact form and sent none of them, because the form kept saying, “Add a subject.” You, meanwhile, left an honest three-star review that said it needed more themes. inkJam read it fourteen times and took notes.

Behind the scenesThe contact and feedback pages check every field before sending, and the Top reviews section on the feedback page shows the best-rated comments. The eleven apologies are fiction. The validation is real.
Scene 6 · The grand gesture

Running to the pricing page

inkJam did what any hero does at the airport: it sprinted to the pricing page. Four plans, laid out like a streaming service: Free, Standard, Premium and, tucked in beside them, one made just for you.

“The Student plan,” it panted. “Because the best stories get written between lectures.” You raised an eyebrow at the price, then at the college-email requirement, then you smiled.

Behind the scenespremium.js draws the plan cards from a small list of plans and remembers the one you choose. The prices are examples and no payment is taken.
Scene 7 · Happily ever after

A name where “Guest” used to be

You signed up. In the corner of the page, where it used to say Guest, your name appeared. The + stopped sending you away and opened a blank page instead. inkJam held its breath.

You typed a title. Then a first line. Somewhere in a tiny inkwell, a quill began to write. The two of you lived happily ever after, one post at a time. (To be continued. Rom-coms always get a sequel.)

Behind the scenesSigned-in users can publish posts in any category, save favourites, send messages and feedback, and choose a plan. Everything is saved in your browser.
